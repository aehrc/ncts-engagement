# National Clinical Terminology Service (NCTS)

The **National Clinical Terminology Service (NCTS)** is Australia’s central service for delivering clinical terminology and related tools. It ensures consistent, interoperable health data across systems and supports national eHealth initiatives.

Key offerings:
- **SNOMED CT-AU**: The Australian edition of SNOMED CT, including local extensions.
- **Australian Medicines Terminology (AMT)**: Standardized medicine names for prescribing and dispensing.
- **Terminology servers**: Hosted services (e.g., Ontoserver) for FHIR-based terminology operations.
- **Syndication**: Access to release content for local hosting.
- **Tools**: Shrimp (browser) to freely and visually explore SNOMED CT-AU, LOINC and other CodeSystems; Snapper (ValueSet editor); and APIs for development purposes (e.g., anonymous direct connection to the NCTS Ontoserver endpoints to undertake exploration and learn the basics) as well as production purposes (e.g., automating the pulldown of monthly release updates of content).

NCTS is overseen by the **Australian Digital Health Agency** with technical and content input from **CSIRO**. It underpins interoperability, analytics, and safe clinical data exchange.

Learn more: [NCTS Portal](https://www.healthterminologies.gov.au/)
