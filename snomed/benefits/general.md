# Benefits of SNOMED CT

SNOMED CT delivers value across the entire healthcare ecosystem by making clinical information consistent, reusable, and interoperable. It supports better care, informed decisions, and global data exchange.

**Who benefits?**
* **Healthcare providers** – Improve patient outcomes, collaborate effectively, and reduce duplication.  
* **Patients** – Receive safer, more coordinated care and gain control over their health information.  
* **Software vendors** – Build solutions on a global standard used in over 80 countries.  
* **Worldwide healthcare** – Enable accurate data sharing across borders for research and innovation.
