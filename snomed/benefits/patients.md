# Benefits for Patients

SNOMED CT improves the quality and safety of care for patients. It ensures clinical data recorded during consultations has a consistent meaning, so everyone involved in care understands the same information.

Patients benefit by:
* Receiving better, more coordinated care.
* Having key health information accessible to all care providers.
* Gaining more control over their health information.
* Working with providers to make informed decisions.

This consistency supports a smoother care journey and better outcomes.

References: [SNOMED International – Value Proposition](https://www.snomed.org/value-proposition)
